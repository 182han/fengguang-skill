# 成品样例

> 每条含两部分：**A 路径**（走项目模板，只写主体句）与 **B 路径**（裸投喂，写全七段）。
> 直接复制 B 路径即可用；A 路径粘到项目提示词框。

---

## 例 1 · 雪山日照金山（`dramatic` / 极冷极淡）

**A 路径（只写这行）**

```
日出时分的锥形雪峰，云海在山腰缓慢翻涌，峰顶刚被第一缕光照亮【山】
```

**B 路径（完整）**

```
A single snow-covered pyramidal peak rising above a slow-moving sea of clouds,
its summit catching the first alpenglow while the valleys below stay in cold blue shadow.
Thin wind-blown snow streams off the ridgeline.
Very slow lateral truck from a high ridge, 200mm telephoto compression,
each ridge separated by its own layer of haze.
Low-angle dawn side-light, warm pink-gold rim on the summit, cold cyan shadow below.
Majestic and quiet. Cinematic color grading, fine 35mm film grain, natural contrast,
cold cyan shadows, controlled highlights, no blown-out sky.
End on the peak holding still as the light creeps down — nothing enters frame.
```

**为什么这样写**：主角=锥形雪峰（专名）；光位=侧逆光低角度；冷底暖顶（数据：极冷极淡板 sat 0.24）；两条运动（云动、镜头横移）但只有一条镜头主轴。

## 例 2 · 沙丘脊线（`cinematic-calm` / 1x 极简）

```
A single curved dune crest slicing diagonally across frame,
wind ripples etched into the sunlit face, the shadowed face falling into deep cool blue.
Absolutely empty. Fine sand grains lifting at the crest in a slow drift.
Static locked-off shot on a tripod, 400mm telephoto, compressed planes.
Low raking side-light just before sunset, extreme contrast between warm crest and cold shadow.
Silent and austere. Near-monochrome palette, one dominant hue,
fine grain, controlled highlights, no blown-out sky.
End on the empty ridge — no footprints, nothing enters frame.
```

**为什么这样写**：负空间 60%+；主角是一条脊线不是沙海；静态机位避免溢出；1x 式的减法。

## 例 3 · 银河拱桥（`cinematic-calm` / 暗底暖星云）

**A 路径**

```
银河拱桥横跨盐湖，湖面如镜倒映星轨，无月夜【星空】
```

**B 路径**

```
The Milky Way arching over a flooded salt flat, water glass-still and mirroring the galactic core,
salt polygons faintly visible under the shallow water. Stars drift imperceptibly in a long-exposure feel.
Ultra-slow dolly-in at knee height, 14mm ultra-wide, horizon perfectly level.
Moonless night, faint zodiacal glow in the east, cold blue ambient with a warm galactic core.
Vast and silent. Deep dark base, luminous warm nebula, magenta and teal,
natural contrast, fine grain.
End on the mirror reflection holding — no camp, no fire, no lights.
```

**注意**：天文题材亮度目标 75–85，比风光片暗一档；`no camp / no fire / no lights` 必须写，否则模型爱加篝火帐篷。

## 例 4 · 深空星云（天文子类 1）

```
A deep-sky nebula field filling the entire frame, ionized hydrogen clouds in magenta and teal,
dark dust lanes threading through the luminous gas, dense star field resolved to pinpoints.
No horizon, no landscape — telescope framing only.
Slow luminance pulse as the gas brightens, camera locked, no parallax.
Deep dark base with high dynamic range, natural contrast, fine grain.
End on the gas holding — no spacecraft, no text, nothing enters frame.
```

**为什么单独成类**：无地景、无焦段概念、色彩是"自发光"而非反射光，和地面风光完全两套语法。

## 例 5 · 秋色峡谷（`dramatic` / 暖浓）

```
A densely forested canyon in peak autumn, ochre and rust canopy filling the mid-ground,
a thin river catching the only cold light at the canyon floor.
Leaves drifting slowly down the slope, mist lifting off the water.
Slow crane up from the river to the canopy, 35mm, deep focus.
Late afternoon side-light raking across the ridge, warm ochre highlights against cool blue shadow.
Rich and quiet. Rich warm palette, ochre and rust, cool shadow separation,
cinematic grade, fine grain, controlled highlights.
End on the canopy holding — nothing enters frame.
```

## 反例与改法

### ❌ 失败写法

```
美丽的雪山日落，4K超高清，电影级画质，震撼大气，无人
```

**六个问题**：
1. 无主角（"雪山"不是一个可命名的峰体）
2. 无光位（"日落"没说侧逆光还是正面光）
3. 质感词会被模板**重复追加六次**
4. `无人` 会与 endingPool 重复
5. 无运镜
6. 无高光控制 → 天空必死白

### ✅ 改后

```
日落最后十分钟的锥形雪峰，侧逆光把峰顶边缘烧成金线，山谷沉入冷蓝，
一缕雪雾被风拉出峰脊【山】
```

**改动**：具体峰体 → 明确光位 → 一个自然运动 → 保留尺度线索（雪雾）→ 删掉全部画质词。

## 批量生成时的差异化技巧

同主题连续多段时，`seed` 会轮转运镜，但池子只有 4 项，第 5 段开始重复。用**主体句差异**承担变化：

| 段 | 主体句差异点 | 运镜 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 峰顶（远） | 横移 |
| 2 | 冰川舌（近） | 推进 |
| 3 | 冰碛湖倒影（低） | 静态 |
| 4 | 雪脊风纹（微距） | 长焦压缩 |
| 5 | 云海翻涌（高） | 航拍俯视 |

这样即使运镜池重复，画面也不会重复。
